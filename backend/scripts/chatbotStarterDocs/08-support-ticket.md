# Contact support and raise a support ticket
Category: Support

If you have a problem with the CRM itself, raise a support ticket to the CRM provider's team.

## Raise a ticket from Settings (Admin users)
1. Open **Settings** and select **Support**.
2. Click **Raise Support Ticket**.
3. Enter a title and description, and choose a **Priority**.
4. Click **Raise Ticket**.

## Raise a ticket from this assistant
In the chat window, click the ticket icon at the top, or use the **Raise a ticket** button that appears when the assistant cannot answer. The ticket form is pre-filled with a summary of your chat. Check it and click **Raise ticket**.

## Track your tickets
In **Settings, Support**, the **My Support Tickets** list shows your previous requests with their priority, created date and status (for example Resolved).

## Tip for faster help
Describe what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and any error message you saw.
