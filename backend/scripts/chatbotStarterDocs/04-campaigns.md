# Create and send a campaign
Category: Templates and Campaigns

A campaign sends a WhatsApp template message to many customers. Campaigns can only use templates that are approved by Meta.

## Create a campaign
1. Open **Campaigns** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Create Campaign**.
3. Under **Campaign Details** enter:
   - **Campaign Name**.
   - **Campaign Type**: Promotional, Broadcast, Follow Up or Announcement.
   - **WhatsApp Template**: select an approved template. Only Meta-approved templates are listed.
4. Fill in **Campaign Variable Content** if your template has variables.
5. Select the audience: choose the customers who should receive it. **Select All** and **Clear All** help with long lists.
6. Optional: use **Schedule Campaign** to pick a date and time. If you leave it empty the campaign stays as a draft.
7. Optional: upload a **Campaign Media** image (JPG, PNG or WEBP, maximum 15 MB).
8. Check the **Campaign Summary** and save.

## Create a campaign with AI
Click **AI Generate** on the Campaigns page. The AI writes a campaign name, type and message for you. Review it, use **Generate Again** if you want another version, and click **Use Campaign** to keep it.

## Send or resend
Open the campaign and use **Send Campaign** to send it to the selected customers. In the campaign details you can use **Resend Campaign** to send it again.

## Edit or delete
Use **Edit Campaign** to change the name, template, audience, schedule or image. Use **Delete Campaign** to remove it.

## Troubleshooting
- **"No approved templates available"**: you must first create a template and wait for Meta to approve it. See "Create a WhatsApp template".
- **Campaign stays as a draft**: a campaign without a schedule is a draft. Send it manually or add a schedule.
