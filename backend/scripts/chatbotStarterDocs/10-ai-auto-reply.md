# AI Auto-Reply for customer chats
Category: Settings

AI Auto-Reply lets the CRM answer YOUR customers' WhatsApp messages automatically. It is available to Admin users in **Settings, AI Auto-Reply**. (This is different from the in-app assistant you are chatting with now, which only helps you use the CRM.)

## Turn it on
1. Open **Settings** and select **AI Auto-Reply**.
2. Switch on **Enable AI auto-reply**. This is the master switch for your whole CRM.
3. Fill in the **Business profile**: business name, what your business does, working hours, languages, location, contact details, and policies and key information.
4. Choose the **Tone of replies** and add any **Extra instructions** (optional).
5. Set **Conversation memory** (how much of the earlier chat the AI remembers).
6. Optional: under **Lead capture** turn on **Create leads automatically** and choose who new leads are assigned to.
7. Save. The page shows "All changes saved" when done.

## Test your setup
Use **Test your setup** to type a sample customer message and see how the AI would answer. It also shows when the AI would hand the chat to your team.

## Live status
The **Live status** section shows whether the AI service is available and whether the last auto-reply failed.

## Troubleshooting
- **AI service shows Unavailable**: the AI service may be temporarily down or not configured. Try again later or raise a support ticket.
- **Replies are wrong or too general**: fill in the business profile and policies in more detail, and add extra instructions.
