# Tasks and customer tickets
Category: Team Work

## Tasks
Open **Tasks** from the sidebar. You can switch between **Table** and **Grid** views. For each task you can update its **status** and **priority**, and you can delete a task you no longer need. The CRM confirms each change with a message such as "Task status updated successfully".

## Customer tickets (inside your CRM)
**Tickets** in the sidebar is for tracking issues raised by YOUR customers. It is different from **Settings, Support**, which is for contacting the CRM provider's support team.

### Create a ticket
1. Open **Tickets**.
2. Click to add a new ticket.
3. Select the **Customer** and, if you want, **Assign Employee**.
4. Enter the **Title** and **Description** ("Describe the issue").
5. Set the **Priority**: LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH.
6. Set the **Status**: OPEN, IN PROGRESS, RESOLVED or CLOSED.
7. Save. The CRM confirms "Ticket Created Successfully".

### Update or delete
Edit a ticket to change its status, priority or assignee. Use **Delete Ticket** to remove it.
