# Create a WhatsApp template
Category: Templates and Campaigns

Templates are the messages you send to customers who have not messaged you recently. Every template must be approved by Meta before you can use it in a campaign.

## Create a template
1. Open **Templates** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Create Template**.
3. Fill in **Basic Information**:
   - **Template Name**: use a clear name, for example `order_confirmation`.
   - **Category**: **Marketing**, **Utility** or **Authentication**. This is the Meta WhatsApp template category.
   - **Purpose**: for example Welcome, Order Confirmation, Order Update, Payment Reminder, Appointment Reminder, Support Follow-up, Festival Greeting, Promotion or Custom.
   - **Language**: for example English (US), English (UK), Tamil or Hindi.
4. Choose a **Header Type**: No Header, Text, Image, Video or Document.
5. Write the message body, and optionally a **Footer** and buttons.
6. Save the template and submit it for approval.

## Template status
After you submit, Meta reviews the template. Its status is shown on the Templates page. Only templates with status **APPROVED** can be used in campaigns.

## If a template is rejected
1. Open the template and choose **Edit Template**.
2. Read the reason shown under "Rejected by Meta".
3. Fix the issue and resubmit it.

## Send a template
Use **Send Template**, select the customers (you can use **Select All** or **Clear All**), and send.
