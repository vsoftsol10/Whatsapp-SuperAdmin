# Integrations
Category: Settings

Integrations connect outside systems to your CRM.

## Add an integration
1. Open **Settings** and select **Integrations**.
2. Click **Add Integration**.
3. Under "What do you want to connect?" pick the provider or type. The **Integration Type** is filled in for you when you pick a provider.
4. Enter an **Integration Name**. This name is only for you, to tell integrations apart.
5. Click **Create Integration**.

## Manage integrations
Connected systems are listed on the Integrations page. Open an integration to see its details and to copy values you need (the CRM shows "Copied to clipboard"). You can also save automation settings or delete an integration you no longer use.
