# Add and view customers
Category: Customers and Leads

## View customers
Open **Customers** from the sidebar to see your customer list. Select a customer to open their profile.

## Add a customer
1. Open **Customers**.
2. Click the button to add a new customer.
3. Fill in the customer details and save.

## Edit a customer
Open the customer profile and use the edit option to update their details.

## Where customers are used
Customers are the people you select as the audience when you create a campaign or send a template.
