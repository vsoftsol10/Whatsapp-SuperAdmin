# Troubleshooting common problems
Category: Troubleshooting

## I cannot select a template when creating a campaign
Only templates approved by Meta appear. Open **Templates** and check the status. If it is not APPROVED, wait for Meta's review. If it was rejected, open **Edit Template**, fix the problem and resubmit.

## My template was rejected
Open the template, choose **Edit Template**, read the reason under "Rejected by Meta", fix it and resubmit. Use a clear template name, a correct category (Marketing, Utility or Authentication) and message text that follows WhatsApp's rules.

## WhatsApp will not connect
Refresh the page and try again from **Settings, Security**. Allow pop-ups for the Meta window. For an existing WhatsApp Business App number, Meta usually needs about 7 days or more of real activity first.

## A campaign did not send
Check that the campaign has an audience, that its template is approved, and that it is not just a draft. A campaign without a schedule stays a draft until you send it. If it still fails, raise a support ticket with the campaign name.

## I do not see some menu items
Employees, Backup & Restore, Support, Audit Logs and AI Auto-Reply are visible to Admin users only. Ask your Admin for access.

## AI Auto-Reply is not answering customers
Check that **Enable AI auto-reply** is on in **Settings, AI Auto-Reply** and look at **Live status** for errors. Fill in your business profile so the AI has information to use.

## The page shows an error or keeps loading
Refresh the page and sign in again. If the problem continues, raise a support ticket and include the page name and what you were doing.
