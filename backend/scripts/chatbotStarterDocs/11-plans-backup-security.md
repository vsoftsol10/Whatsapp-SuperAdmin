# Plans, backup, password and employees
Category: Settings

## Change your password
Open **Settings, Security** and click **Change password** in the Password card. If you forgot your password, use the forgot password link on the login page and follow the email.

## Plans and subscription
Open **Settings** and go to **Plans** to see **Available Plans**, including the Trial plan and what each plan includes under Features. Use **Choose Plan** to upgrade. If you have a question about billing, raise a support ticket.

## Backup and Restore (Admin)
Open **Settings, Backup & Restore** to see your backup history and manage your backups. If a backup fails, an error message is shown on that page. Raise a support ticket if it keeps failing.

## Employees (Admin)
Admin users see **Employees** in the sidebar. Use it to view your team members and to add, edit or view an employee.

## Audit Logs (Admin)
**Settings, Audit Logs** shows a history of important actions taken in your CRM.
