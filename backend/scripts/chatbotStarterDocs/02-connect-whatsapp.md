# Connect your WhatsApp number
Category: Getting Started

You connect WhatsApp in **Settings, Security**, in the **Connected channels** section. The WhatsApp card there shows your connection status.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings** from the sidebar and select **Security**.
2. In **Connected channels**, find the WhatsApp card.
3. Choose one of the two options:
   - **Connect with New Number**: use a phone number that is not already active in the WhatsApp Business App.
   - **Connect Existing WhatsApp Business App**: connect a number you already use in the WhatsApp Business App.
4. Follow the Meta (Facebook) window that opens and sign in when asked.
5. Select your business and phone number and finish the steps.
6. When it is done the card shows your account as connected and your WhatsApp data starts syncing.

## Disconnect
On the same card use the disconnect option and confirm when asked.

## Common problems
- **Facebook window does not open or the page says the Facebook SDK failed to load**: refresh the page and try again. Make sure pop-ups and ad blockers are not blocking the Meta window.
- **Connecting an existing WhatsApp Business App number fails**: Meta usually wants the number to have had at least about 7 days of real activity in the WhatsApp Business App first. Try again after the number has been used for some days, or use **Connect with New Number**.
- **Connected but old chats are missing**: if the business declines to share chat history, or the history sync does not finish, only new messages appear. New messages will still come through normally.
- **The number was recognised as already active in the WhatsApp Business App**: Meta may connect it as an existing app number automatically.
