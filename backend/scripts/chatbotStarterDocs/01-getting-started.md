# Getting started and finding your way around Vatup CRM
Category: Getting Started

The left sidebar is the main menu of Vatup CRM. It contains:

- **Dashboard**: an overview of your CRM activity.
- **Conversations**: three sub-items, **WhatsApp**, **Facebook** and **Instagram**, one for each channel.
- **Customers**: your customer list.
- **Leads**: potential customers you are following up.
- **Campaigns**: bulk WhatsApp messages sent using an approved template.
- **Templates**: WhatsApp message templates that must be approved by Meta.
- **Tasks**: work items for you and your team.
- **Tickets**: customer issues you track inside your CRM.
- **Employees**: team members (visible to Admin users only).
- **Settings**: security and channel connections, integrations, plans, support and more.

## Settings sections
Inside **Settings** you will find: **Security**, **Integrations**, **Backup & Restore**, **Support**, **Audit Logs** and **AI Auto-Reply**. Backup & Restore, Support, Audit Logs and AI Auto-Reply are available to Admin users only.

## First steps for a new account
1. Connect your WhatsApp number (see "Connect your WhatsApp number").
2. Create and submit a message template (see "Create a WhatsApp template").
3. Add your customers or leads.
4. Send a campaign once your template is approved.
