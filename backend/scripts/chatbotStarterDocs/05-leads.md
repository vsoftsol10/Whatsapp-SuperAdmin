# Manage leads
Category: Customers and Leads

Leads are people who may become customers.

## Add a lead
1. Open **Leads** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Lead**.
3. Fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Phone Number** and **Company**.
4. Choose the **Source**: Website, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or Referral.
5. Optional: use **Assign Employee** to give the lead to a team member, or leave it Unassigned.
6. Set the **Status**: New, Contacted, Qualified or Won.
7. Write the lead's **Requirements** and save.

## Update a lead
Open the lead from the list to view it, then use the edit option to change the status, owner or details.

## Convert a lead to a customer
When a lead becomes a customer, use the convert option on the lead. The CRM confirms "Lead converted to customer successfully" and the person appears under **Customers**.

## Delete a lead
Use **Delete Lead** and confirm.

## Automatic leads
If an Admin turns on lead capture in **Settings, AI Auto-Reply**, leads can be created automatically from WhatsApp chats. See "AI Auto-Reply for customer chats".
